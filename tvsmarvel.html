TVS Ntorq X MARVEL

TVS partnered with Marvel Comics, the legendary publisher behind iconic superheroes, to unveil three new exciting superhero-themed variants of the Ntorq scooter. To advertise this Super Squad Edition, we created four Key Visuals inspired by the Marvel superhero films and posters.

Creative Brief:

Introduce the TVS Ntorq 125 SuperSquad Edition, inspired by Marvel superheroes, combining smart technology with an epic riding experience.

Target Audience:

Gen Z consumers, young urban riders who are Marvel enthusiasts & who crave a performance scooter that reflects their personality: bold, stylish, and tech-savvy.

Key Message:

Ride like a superhero—TVS Ntorq 125 SuperSquad Edition lets you embody your favorite Marvel characters with cutting-edge features and striking designs.

Tone of Voice:

Energetic, youthful, and adventurous. Bold and aspirational language, capturing the confidence and power of Marvel superheroes and the thrill of riding.

Brochure:

To further extend the campaign, TVS tied up with a few influencers, cosplaying in their favourite superhero costumes to run creatives on digital. Their idea was to show how the Ntorq was a superhero extension of the self.
